1.5 Terms of Service API: GET /api/v1/auth/terms-of-service
1.6 Privacy Policy API: GET /api/v1/auth/privacy-policy
1.6.1 Child Safety Standards API: GET /child-safety (or /child-safety-standards, /child-safety-policy, /api/v1/auth/child-safety, /api/v1/general/child-safety)
- Description: Returns WithMe24 Child Safety Standards policy document (Adults Only 18+ policy, zero tolerance for CSAE/CSAM, reporting, enforcement, and support contact). Pass ?format=html for rendered HTML content.
